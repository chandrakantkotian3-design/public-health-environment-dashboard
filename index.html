04 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Simple data. Better understanding.

This educational project demonstrates how public health and environmental survey data can be transformed into readable charts, trends and community insights. The first six records come from the original survey export; future website submissions are added to the same dataset.

Survey responses are self-reported and should not be treated as official medical, environmental or crime statistics.
